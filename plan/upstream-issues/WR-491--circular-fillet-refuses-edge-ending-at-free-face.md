#### WR-491. `SmApiCircularFillet` refuses a solid whose filleted edge terminates at a free face (cliff-end rail-rail interpolation drops zero UV curves)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Fillets
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp:7024`, `SmFilletEdge::CalcRailRailInterpolation` (def. `:6666`); surfaced through `SmFilletCorner::CalcCornerEdgeGeom` (`SmFilletCorner.cpp:1637`) and `SmFilletExecutive::CreateFilletBrep` (`SmFilletExecutive.cpp:2359`).
- **What happens:** When a filleted (junction) edge terminates at a free face of the solid, the corner at that free end builds a rail-rail interpolation corner edge (`SM_FE_RAIL_RAIL_INTERPOLATION`). Its 3-D interpolation curve is projected onto the corner's original face with `pSurf->DropCurve(...)`; for this geometry `DropCurve` returns **zero** UV curves, and the `sUVCurves.GetSize() == 1` guard raises `SM_ERR`. Because the edge type is not `SM_FE_FILLET_X_SIDE_FACE`, the global-merge forgiveness in `SmFilletCorner::CalcCornerEdgeGeom` does not apply, so the error propagates out of `SmApiCircularFillet` as status 1001 for every radius. (At one tested axis placement the failure does not trigger — see trigger note.)
- **Trigger:** Box 100x100x10 unioned with a cylinder r=20 h=20 whose axis lies on the plate edge (x=100, half the cylinder overhangs); fillet r=2, all edges. Confirmed with a standalone driver (box + `SmApiBooleanUnion` + `SmApiCircularFillet`): axis x=100 and x=105 fail with 1001, x=50 (no overhang) succeeds; x=110 happened to succeed in the driver, so the exact placement modulates which corner path is taken — the trigger is the junction edge ending on the plate side face, not the radius.
- **How found:** runtime-instrumented driver (debug prints at each `SER(SM_ERR)` candidate in the fillet path, rebuilt `SMLib` incrementally; sources reverted after the run).
- **Confirmed:** yes, v1.0.1 commit 3fd5b95, Debian 12 / gcc 12, driver `/issue-1410` of the filing fork; OCCT 7.6.3 rounds the same solid.
